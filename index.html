Oranges

Oranges Possums and Ants is a foundational design portfolio completed during the first year of my undergraduate degree in the class "Foundations of Design Representation".

The portfolio centres around design representation and contains; photography, hand sketching, 2D digital sketching and 3D display models created to scale in CAD software Rhinoceros 3D.

The portfolio graphically displays the history of oranges, animal's interaction with the fruit and general exploration of design principles.

Assist Chair

The Assistance Chair portfolio was completed as my Units 3 and 4 Product Design project. In this subject I received a 48 raw score to contribute to my ATAR.

The project focused on accessibility and ergonomics to create a chair to assist an individual with a disability and their caregiver. The chair was designed, modelled and built.

The portfolio consists of a rigorous production journal, Gantt chart and modifications log, all leading to the completion of the Assistance Chair.

Rear Wing

The Model DRS portfolio was completed as my Units 3 and 4 Systems Engineering project.

Derived from my interest in the engineering aspects of Formula 1, I designed, tested and modelled a rear wing of an F1 car with a pneumatic actuator. The wing followed the FIA's sporting regulations.

The final result was a resin 3D-printed static model focused on aerodynamic elements, and an actuating carbon fibre section model focused on motion and electronic integration.

Interactive

Surfaces & Shapes follows on from the foundational design work completed in Oranges Possums and Ants ("Oranges"), focusing on my technical skill with parametric design completed in Rhinoceros 3D.

The portfolio works on iterating on panels and grids to produce a render acting as a metaphor for an orange surface.

Stylistic elements and a command of lighting were a major point of importance in the renders.

Possums

If Possums Roamed Professors Walk centres around the idea of rewilding urban spaces, particularly a prominent location on the University of Melbourne campus. Built from biodiversity metrics and design ideation, a form is created to promote possum rewilding on campus.

The form is built upon the BIG Pavilion, where its structural elements informed the entire design.

Rhinoceros 3D was used to model the design in 3D, with 2D drawings being extracted from this and presented as architectural drawings. A narrative sequence is then formed following these design drawings.

Diagramming

The Diagramming portfolio was the first of three portfolios completed in my second-year architecture elective. All three centre around the precedent design DHY House in the Thai Nguyen region of Vietnam.

The portfolio focuses on representing the house in various aspects, with site and context, shadow, massing, programmatic and circulation diagrams being completed.

2D diagrams were completed in Affinity Designer, and all 3D diagram linework was derived from my Rhinoceros 3D model. Ladybug was used for the shadow study.

Computation

The Computation portfolio is the second of three portfolios completed in the architecture elective "Design Processes and Techniques". The portfolio progresses from the Diagramming portfolio to look at parametric variations of the DHY House precedent.

Grasshopper was used alongside Rhinoceros 3D to produce variations of the model. Ladybug was then used to complete solar analysis.

The portfolio includes the Grasshopper script for angling roof volumes and shaping and angling louvres.

Prototyping

The Prototyping portfolio is the final portfolio in the architecture elective "Design Processes and Techniques", following Diagramming and Computation.

The portfolio consists of 2D linework extracted from a Rhinoceros 3D model of the DHY House section I designed, and the stages of building a 3D prototype with 3D-printed facade pieces.

The final element is renders completed in Twinmotion, with post-production in Affinity Designer and Affinity Photo.

Cyberstar

The Cyberstar portfolio is the design document for this website.

It includes web design research, stylescape iteration, a sitemap, work on typography, site wireframes and an HTML work in progress completed on Neocities, with the main pieces being the Home page, Portfolio page and Timeline showcases.

These acted as mock-ups, being an accumulation of all my design choices, and they are what this site is derived from.
